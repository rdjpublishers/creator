About Me

Building tools that save time.
Writing tech that makes sense.

Founder of RDJ Publishers · Based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India · Building since 2025

Hi, I'm Ratheesh. I build free web tools, games, and utilities that anyone can use — no sign-up, no paywalls, no nonsense. Everything I ship is tested by me personally before it goes live.

My studio, RDJ Publishers, started in 2025 with a simple idea: practical browser tools should be free and private. Every tool processes your data locally — nothing ever leaves your device.

I also write about tech — covering topics I actually care about and research thoroughly.
